Features

Adinkra Splash — Osrane ne Nsoromma
The crescent moon glides in from the right, the morning star from the left. They embrace at centre, a gold halo blooms, and the names fade up in Tangerine script. Tap the button to enter — no auto-dismiss.
How it works
Pure CSS keyframes. The moon SVG is a filled disc with a smaller ivory disc offset to carve the crescent (same technique used in the OG card). Star is a 10-point path. Halo is a radial-gradient with mix-blend-mode:multiply for the warm bloom against the ivory backdrop.
Live Countdown
Days / hours / minutes / seconds down to 2027-04-16 09:00 GMT.
How it works
JS setInterval, clamps at zero (no negatives). Target: new Date('2027-04-16T09:00:00+00:00').getTime().
Traditional 5-Step Programme
Vertical gold-dotted timeline covering the arc of a Ghanaian engagement: Arrival → Welcome → Knocking & Requests → Blessings & Exchange → Reception.
How it works
Static HTML timeline styled with a central gold gradient line, gold-glow dots and Cinzel timestamps. To edit the programme, edit the .prog-item blocks in index.html.
Landmark-First Directions
Big "Near Christ Ambassador School" callout above the map — the digital equivalent of the direction you'd give a friend on the phone. Google Maps embed centred on Christ Ambassador School, GhanaPost Digital Address (GA-536-1756) displayed as a dashed-gold badge, "Open in Maps" CTA.
How it works
Iframe embed: maps.google.com/maps?q=Christ%20Ambassador%20School%2C%20South%20Odorkor%20Estate with mild sepia/saturation filter to match the gold palette. Open-in-Maps CTA uses the digital address directly for pin-drop.
RSVP Form
Name (required), phone (optional, admin-only), guest count (1–6+), side (Bride's / Groom's / A friend to both), free-form note. Live "N guests have confirmed · M people expected" ticker at the bottom.
How it works
POST writes to data/rsvp.json via POST /api/rsvp.php. Public GET returns only counts (not names/phones/notes) — the ticker never leaks identity. Admin sees everything.

Limits: 800 RSVPs cap, 20-second rate limit per IP, 60-char name, 25-char phone, 300-char note.
Wishes Wall Carousel
Visitors leave a short blessing. Wall renders as a paginated carousel: 3 cards per slide on desktop, 2 on tablet, 1 on mobile. Prev/next arrows, dot pagination, touch swipe. Same pattern as Charles Taylor.
How it works
POST /api/wishes.php appends to data/wishes.json. Public GET is capped at 50 newest, with ?all=1 for full fetch if we ever need it.

Limits: 1000 wishes cap, 20-second rate limit per IP, 60-char name, 300-char message.
Background Music
Same happy instrumental track used by Baby Coleman and Baby Nortey — VibeLink brand-consistency for joyful family events. Auto-starts on first tap of the splash.
How it works
File: music.mp3 (3.5 MB, MP3 256 kbps). Copied from Coleman on deploy. Toggle button top-right.
Custom OG Share Card
1200×630 gold-on-ivory JPEG with the crescent moon embracing a star + "Frank & Hannah" in Tangerine script + date + venue. Under 42 KB — well under WhatsApp's 300 KB ceiling.
How it works
Server-side ImageMagick pipeline (see the deployment section). Uses Tangerine (script for names), Cinzel (date band), Cormorant Garamond Italic (invitation copy).
Family Contacts
Two direct-dial cards: Pastor Michael (0244 721 662), Mary (0541 770 126).
How it works
Standard <a href="tel:..."> links. Grid auto-fits.
WhatsApp Share FAB
Floating green button (bottom-right). One tap opens WhatsApp with a pre-written invitation and the page URL. Recipient sees the OG preview in the chat.

Server-Side Protections

  • Rate limit: 20 seconds per IP per feature
  • Hard caps: 800 RSVPs, 1000 wishes
  • File locks on writes (no corruption from concurrent submissions)
  • Public RSVP GET returns counts only — no names, phones, or notes exposed publicly
  • /data/ directory blocked at nginx level
  • Admin key compared with hash_equals() (timing-safe)
  • All text inputs sanitized (control chars stripped, length-capped)

Design Notes & History

Zero photographs, by design. Frank & Hannah declined to use any real photos of themselves. The entire visual identity was built around the Adinkra symbol Osrane ne Nsoromma (The Moon and the Star) — the traditional Akan emblem for love, faithfulness, and harmony in marriage. It appears on the splash (star + crescent embrace animation), as hero ornament, in section dividers, on the OG card, and in the footer.
Landmark-first directions. The bride's home is on 14 Cosmos Street, South Odorkor Estate — hard to find for outsiders. Instead of leading with a Google Map, we lead with a big "Near Christ Ambassador School" callout — the direction the family would give on the phone. The map centres on the school; the digital address (GA-536-1756) is displayed as a dashed-gold badge for pin-drop.
Music track is shared. The music.mp3 file is a copy of the same happy VibeLink track used by Coleman and Nortey (identical bytes, 3.5 MB, 256 kbps MP3). If the family ever wants a different track, drop it in and rename to music.mp3 — no code change needed.
NEVER recreate a wildcard cert *.vibelinkevent.com without DNS-01 automation. Use per-subdomain HTTP-01 certs — they auto-renew.
