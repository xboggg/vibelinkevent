Two Modes — Save-the-Date vs Full Invitation

Right now the public sees the Save-the-Date tease only. The full invitation (venues, RSVP, wishes wall, officiant contacts, footer verse) is fully built and live on the server, but hidden behind a URL gate so it doesn't steal thunder from the formal invitation you'll send later.
What guest seesURL to give them
Save-the-Date tease — splash (hands + rings) → tap → small ivory card with names, date, live countdown, "full invitation to follow", and 3 calendar buttons (Google / Apple / Outlook). WhatsApp share link at the bottom. Music still plays. https://ericsherita.vibelinkevent.com/
Full invitation — splash → full-bleed hero → invitation body → ceremony & reception cards → RSVP form → wishes wall → officiant & family contacts → footer verse. Same site, gate lifted. https://ericsherita.vibelinkevent.com/?invite=honour

How the gate works

A single flag at the top of the <script> block in index.html:

const FORCE_FULL = false;
const _params = new URLSearchParams(window.location.search);
const isFullInvite = FORCE_FULL || _params.get('invite') === 'honour';
if (!isFullInvite){
  document.body.classList.add('teaser-mode');
}

CSS then hides .hero, all .chapter sections, the footer, and the WhatsApp share FAB when body.teaser-mode is set, and reveals .teaser-panel instead.

Flipping the whole site to full invitation (when the formal invitation goes out)

Two ways:

  1. Recommended: keep sharing the ?invite=honour URL and the tease stays live for anyone who hits the bare domain. Zero code change.
  2. Flip it publicly: in index.html, change const FORCE_FULL = false; to const FORCE_FULL = true; and re-upload. Every visitor now sees the full invitation, no matter what URL they use. This is the "wedding week" move.
The gate is a social wall, not a security wall. Anyone with the ?invite=honour URL can forward it. For a wedding invitation that's the right level (guests do forward invitations, and that's fine), but do not treat this like a password. If you'd rather have a real passcode field, ask — we can wire one on the splash instead.
OG card + meta title are the same in both modes. A WhatsApp preview from either URL shows the same sage-and-gold "Save the Date" card. Fine now; when you flip to the full invitation phase you may want to regenerate the OG card so it reads "You're Invited" instead of "Save the Date". Not urgent.

Features

Cinematic Splash — Hands & Rings
Full-bleed close-up of hands with the gold bands on ivory lace and eucalyptus. Sage/ivory card with gold-cornered frame fades up over a dark vignette; a diamond gold divider crowns the "SAVE THE DATE" overline, then the names and date animate in. Tap the button to enter — no auto-dismiss.
How it works
Splash background scales from 1.14 → 1.0 over 12s (slow reveal). The card is a translucent overlay with a backdrop blur, keyframe-driven fade-and-lift, and two absolutely-positioned corner brackets. Uses splash.jpg / splash-medium.jpg / splash-mobile.jpg via CSS media queries so mobile pulls a 52 KB variant, not the 231 KB desktop image.
Full-Bleed Golden-Hour Hero
The couple walk hand-in-hand through a soft golden-hour path. Wide right-side gradient tucks the type-block into readable territory without obscuring the couple. Great Vibes script names with a gold Cormorant italic ampersand — Vogue-editorial typography.
How it works
Ken Burns "breath" animation loops scale(1 → 1.05 → 1) over 22s. Desktop → right-anchored panel. Tablet/mobile → bottom-anchored with 180° gradient so type stays legible against the sky. Three image variants (mobile / medium / full).
Live Countdown
Days / hours / minutes / seconds down to Saturday, 28 November 2026 at 12PM Eastern. Numbers zero-pad for typographic rhythm; four sage-tinted glass tiles under the hero name-block.
How it works
JS setInterval at 1s. Target: new Date('2026-11-28T17:00:00+00:00').getTime() (12PM ET = 17:00 UTC — the site does not shift when daylight savings changes because it's anchored to a fixed UTC instant). Clamps at zero (no negatives).
One-Tap Add-to-Calendar
Three pill buttons on ivory: Google Calendar (URL-based), Apple Calendar and Outlook / Other (both download the same .ics file). Designed for the US-based Ghanaian diaspora who'll want it on their phone the moment they see it.
How it works
Google uses the standard calendar.google.com/calendar/render?action=TEMPLATE deep-link. Apple/Outlook use a client-side Blob with a hand-rolled VCALENDAR — no server round-trip, no library. Both venues + times are embedded in the DESCRIPTION field.
Ceremony & Reception Cards
Two elegant address cards side by side on desktop, stacked on mobile: Potter's Love Assemble of God (12PM) and The Sapphire Grand (5PM). Gold corner brackets, sage circular icon, Cinzel time badge.
How it works
Static markup — edit .detail-card blocks in index.html. Full addresses and directions come with the formal invitation later; this Save-the-Date deliberately only teases the venues.
RSVP Form (diaspora-first)
Full name (required), email (required), phone (optional), Joyfully Yes / Sadly No toggle, guest count (1–6+), side (Bride's / Groom's / A friend to both), ceremony/reception opt-in, free-form note (dietary, well wishes). Live "N guests have confirmed · M people expected" ticker at the bottom.
How it works
POST writes to data/rsvp.json via POST /api/rsvp.php. Public GET returns only counts — no names, emails, phones, or notes exposed. Choosing "Sadly No" collapses the guest-count and side fields; the form still accepts a note (so guests can send love from afar).

Limits: 800 RSVPs cap, 20-second rate limit per IP, 60-char name, 100-char email, 25-char phone, 300-char note.
Wishes Wall Carousel
Visitors leave a short blessing. Wall renders as a paginated carousel: 3 cards per slide on desktop, 2 on tablet, 1 on mobile. Prev/next arrows, dot pagination, touch swipe. Same pattern as Charles Taylor & Frank & Hannah.
How it works
POST /api/wishes.php appends to data/wishes.json. Public GET is capped at 50 newest, with ?all=1 for a full pull when we need to export.

Limits: 1500 wishes cap, 20-second rate limit per IP, 60-char name, 300-char message.
Background Music
Same happy instrumental track used by Baby Coleman and Baby Nortey — VibeLink brand-consistency for joyful family events. Auto-starts on first tap of the splash. Volume defaults to 30 %; toggle top-right.
How it works
File: music.mp3 (3.5 MB, MP3 256 kbps). Copied from Coleman on deploy. If Eric & Sherita ever want their own track, drop it in and rename to music.mp3 — no code change needed.
Custom OG Share Card
1200×630 sage-green panel on an ivory-warm gradient with a gold diamond divider, "Eric & Sherita" in Great Vibes script, "WEDDING INVITATION" in Cinzel small-caps, "SATURDAY · 28 NOV 2026 · 12PM" in the date band. 37 KB — well under WhatsApp's 300 KB ceiling.

Contact Cards
Two dignified cards with gold corner brackets: Pastor Williams Obeng (officiating pastor) and Family Coordinator (RSVP / enquiries). Each card has a Call button (sage) and a WhatsApp button (green) that opens with a pre-written message.
How it works

WhatsApp Share FAB
Floating green button (bottom-right). One tap opens WhatsApp with a pre-written invitation and the page URL. Recipient sees the OG preview in the chat.

Server-Side Protections

  • Rate limit: 20 seconds per IP per feature
  • Hard caps: 800 RSVPs, 1500 wishes
  • File locks on writes (no corruption from concurrent submissions)
  • Public RSVP GET returns counts only — no names, emails, phones, or notes exposed publicly
  • /data/ directory blocked at nginx level (returns 403)
  • Admin key compared with hash_equals() (timing-safe)
  • All text inputs sanitized (control chars stripped, length-capped)
  • Email + phone validated with FILTER_VALIDATE_EMAIL and a numeric-only regex

Deployment

  1. Make changes locally

    Edit files in vibelink/events/ericsherita/.

  2. Upload the HTML

    scp "vibelink/events/ericsherita/index.html" \
    root@38.242.195.0:/var/www/ericsherita.vibelinkevent.com/

    For API files: scp api/*.php root@38.242.195.0:/var/www/ericsherita.vibelinkevent.com/api/

  3. Fix ownership if needed

    ssh root@38.242.195.0 "chown -R www-data:www-data /var/www/ericsherita.vibelinkevent.com/"

  4. Regenerate OG image (if date/venue changes)

    Server-side ImageMagick pipeline. Fonts: Cinzel + Cormorant at /usr/share/fonts/truetype/charlestaylor/, Great Vibes at /usr/share/fonts/truetype/greatvibes/.

  5. Force WhatsApp / Facebook to re-scrape the OG card

    Paste the URL into Facebook Sharing Debugger and click "Scrape Again". WhatsApp caches OG images aggressively — this is the only reliable way to bust it.

Design Notes & History

Palette lifted straight from the paper card. Sage green #7d8e6d, sage deep #5a6b4c, ivory #f5f2e8, warm gold #c9a24a. No design guessing — every colour on the site (including the OG card) was pulled from the couple's physical Save-the-Date so the digital version feels like the same object.
Splash → Hero storytelling. Eric & Sherita supplied two hero images: hands with gold rings on ivory lace, and the couple walking through a golden-hour path. Instead of choosing, we used both — intimate close-up as the splash, full-bleed grand reveal as the hero. That flow is deliberate: the tap moves the viewer from a detail-shot to a wide shot, the same way a wedding film opens.
Countdown is anchored to a fixed UTC instant. 2026-11-28T17:00:00+00:00 = 12PM Eastern Standard Time (November is past DST). Do not "fix" the offset when the US crosses DST in March 2026 — the target date is already in EST, and the fixed-UTC anchor is correct.
Placeholder phone number. Both contact cards use +1 732 794 0000. Update in index.html (three occurrences: two tel: links and two wa.me/ links) before the invitation goes wide.
Music track is shared. The music.mp3 file is the same happy VibeLink track used by Coleman and Nortey (identical bytes, 3.5 MB, 256 kbps MP3). If the couple later wants their own song, drop it in and rename to music.mp3 — no code change needed.
NEVER recreate a wildcard cert *.vibelinkevent.com without DNS-01 automation. Use per-subdomain HTTP-01 certs — they auto-renew. See SSL renewal — vibelinkevent server in memory.
