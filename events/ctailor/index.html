— TRANSITION —

It is with deep sorrow but humble submission to the will of the Almighty that the Taylor, Ayettey, Coffie and Okantey families of Osu Alata, together with the Okang Tsuru family of Accra and Teshie, and the Nanor Kokotey family of the Pobee, Canacoo, Nanor, Darlington and Darkinson families of Fiergbedu, Ada Foah, announce the passing of their beloved son, husband, father, brother and uncle —

Charles Nii Aryertey Taylor,

who answered the final call on Tuesday, 17th March 2026, at the age of 55.

"A great tree has fallen in Osu Alata."
