A Life Beneath the Heavens

His Journey

Nana Kwame Boakye Ofori ruled the people of Akrofuom for thirty-one years with a quiet, listening kind of leadership. He read the night sky like other men read the Bible — and never tired of telling his grandchildren which star was which.

Trained as a surveyor before he was enstooled, he founded the Akrofuom Astronomy Club in 1979 — a small group of teachers and pupils who would gather on the football pitch on moonless nights to watch the planets rise.

He leaves behind his wife of 52 years, Madam Comfort Adoma, five children, fourteen grandchildren, and an entire town that knew where to find him on a clear night.

"The stars do not die," he often said. "They only travel further than we can see."
