Three Beats of His Life

His Rhythm

1.

THE FIRST BEAT

Born into the Adjei drumming lineage of Osu, he was given his first drumstick at age four. By twelve he was drumming at the Homowo festival. By twenty he had inherited the apentemma drum from his grandfather.

2.

THE LONG BEAT

For fifty-seven years he served as the chief drummer of the Osu Mantse. Every state occasion, every funeral of note, every Homowo from 1969 onwards — his hands were on the lead drum. He never missed a single ceremony.

3.

THE FINAL BEAT

He trained twenty-three apprentices, three of whom now serve as state drummers in their own right. His final performance — at the Centenary of the Osu Mantse in October 2025 — was filmed and is now in the National Museum.
