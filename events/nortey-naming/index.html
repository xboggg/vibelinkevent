The Naming Tradition

A beautiful Ghanaian celebration

In Ghanaian culture, the naming ceremony (Outdooring) is one of the most cherished traditions. It marks the formal introduction of a newborn to the family and community, filled with prayers, blessings, and the joyous announcement of the baby's name.

The Outdooring

A cherished Ghanaian tradition where the newborn is formally presented to family and friends, welcoming the little one into the world with joy, love, and celebration.

The Naming

The elder performs sacred rituals, touching the baby's lips with water and wine to teach the difference between truth and falsehood, then reveals the chosen name.

The Blessings

Family and friends shower the newborn with prayers, gifts, and words of wisdom, welcoming the little one into a community of love and support.
