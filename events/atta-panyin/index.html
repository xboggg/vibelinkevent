Official Announcement

It is with deep sorrow but humble acceptance of God's will that the Akua Amankwaa Bretuo Family of Begoro in the Eastern Region announce the passing of their beloved Abusuapanin, Mr. Wilson Atta Krofah (Atta Panin).

Mr. Krofah passed away on Thursday, February 19, 2026 at The Bank Hospital, Accra, Ghana after a short illness. He was 86 years old.
