A Sailor's Story

His voyage

Joseph Nii Tetteh Quaye joined the Ghana Navy in 1969 as a midshipman of nineteen and retired thirty-five years later as a captain in command of the GNS Achimota. In between he served on three frigates, two patrol vessels, and once — famously — refused to abandon a sinking dredger off Saltpond until every man on board was accounted for.

He was awarded the Star of Volta in 1991 for his role in coordinating maritime relief after the Saltpond disaster. His commanding officer wrote in his commendation: "Lieutenant Quaye treats his crew as he treats his children — with the same exacting standards and the same quiet love."

He retired to Tema, where he kept a small fishing boat called Comfort II after his late wife. He fished every morning until his last week of life. His four children, twelve grandchildren, and an entire generation of naval officers he mentored remember him with awe and tenderness alike.
