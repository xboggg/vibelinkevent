A joining of two families

Two Families, One Bond

By the grace of the Almighty and with the blessing of our families, we invite you to witness the traditional marriage ceremony of our children — Frank and Hannah — as our two families come together in love and celebration.

Your presence, your prayers, and your blessings would mean the world to us on this joyful morning.

The Programme

The Order of the Day

7:30 AM

Arrival of the Groom's Family

The groom's family (Abusua) arrives at the bride's home to be received.

8:00 AM

Opening & Welcome

Prayer, welcome, and introduction of the two families.

8:30 AM

The Knocking & Requests

The groom's family formally asks for the hand of the bride, presenting the traditional items.

9:30 AM

Blessings & Exchange

Family blessings, exchange of rings, and the sealing of the union.

10:30 AM

Reception & Refreshments

Food, drink, laughter, and photographs with the families and guests.
