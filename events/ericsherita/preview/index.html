Concept 1 · Premium v8 · RECOMMENDED
The Envelope Opens — final polish
Three real fixes over v7: (1) Dove now flies the full viewport — rewrote the broken nested-rotation orbit into a single manual keyframe path that traces a wide oval around the whole screen (32s loop, flips direction at halfway). No more stuck-at-top dove. (2) Envelope stays visible after the card emerges — softly blurred and at 70% opacity, still readable as background context (not vanished into black). (3) Corner ornaments simplified — single delicate leaf with curving stem and gold-bud tip per corner, instead of the heavy multi-leaf cluster. Frames the card without competing with the text.

The Continuation Card — extends the v8 story

Teaser 2
The Cinematic Stage
Dark backdrop (darker than the splash wood), single soft spotlight from above pulsing gently. No card at all — content floats in the light beam like an opening credits sequence. Names in massive Great Vibes, sequenced fade-ins for each element. Countdown is a single line of huge numerals separated by gold dots ("146 · 03 · 46 · 27") with the labels almost hidden. Calendar buttons are minimal gold-outlined rectangles that shimmer on hover. Feels editorial, Vogue-magazine, quiet and confident. Best if the couple wanted the guest to feel the date rather than use the site.

The Kept Envelope
The v8 envelope stays on screen from the splash, resting on the wood, flap closed with a fresh sage wax seal stamped "R.S.V.P. E&S" instead of the "E & S" monogram.
