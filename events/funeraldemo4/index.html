A Letter to My Family

In Loving Memory of
Pastor Daniel Owusu-Ansah
1951 — 2026
Papa, on his 70th birthday

To my dearest family,

If you are reading this, then the Lord has called me home. Do not weep too long. I have lived a full life — three decades preaching at Calvary, four children grown into the people I prayed for, and your mother by my side for forty-six years. What more could a man ask for?

I want to tell you what I would have said over Sunday lunch if I had been given one more visit. So I asked my friend Kofi to type this letter, and to share it with you on this page.

To Adwoa — keep teaching. The classroom is your pulpit. To Kwame — be patient with your children the way I tried to be with you. To Esi and Yaw — look after your mother. She gave up her dreams so you could chase yours.

And here, my dear friends, my dear congregation — there will be space below for your own words. Tell my family what I meant to you, if I meant anything at all. Tell them a story I never told them. A pastor's family rarely gets to see what he meant to his flock. Let them see now.
