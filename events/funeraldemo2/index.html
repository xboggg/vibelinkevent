IIThe Story
A Life on the Water

She sold fish before the sun rose

For forty-two years, Esi Mansa Dapaah walked the same path from her stall at Elmina market to the canoe landing — bargaining, blessing, scolding, feeding. Every fisherman in town owed her money at least once, and every fisherman in town paid her back.

She raised nine children and three orphans. None went without. Her stall became a chapel, a courtroom, and a soup kitchen by turns. When she retired in 2018, the Chief Fisherman declared a half-day of mourning — she was that essential.
