The Three Pillars of His Reign

His Life

I

The Scholar

Born in Agave-Afedume in 1939, Togbe Wenya VII trained as a teacher at Akatsi Training College, then read history at Legon. His thesis on pre-colonial Ewe kingdoms is still cited today.

II

The King

Enstooled in 1981 after his uncle's passing, he ruled for forty-five years with calm authority. He brought electricity to seventeen villages, built three schools, and ended a sixty-year land dispute with neighbouring Ave.

III

The Father

To his eleven children — and the dozens of others he helped raise — he was simply Tata. He insisted every child finish secondary school. None disobeyed.
